Where to stay

The 3 neighborhoods worth it

  • Downtown / 6th Street — walkable to everything, loudest at night. Best if you want to be in the middle of it.
  • South Congress (SoCo) — boutique hotels, walkable shops and food. Best all-rounder for a first visit.
  • East Austin — best food scene, slightly cheaper stays. Best if you're here to eat.

Booking traps to dodge

  • Always check the total with taxes & fees before you commit — Austin hotels love a $30–45/night "amenity fee."
  • Downtown parking runs $40–60/night. If you're mostly ridesharing, skip the rental car.
  • Free cancellation date matters more than the nightly rate — plans change.

Friday

Arrive + ease in

  • Evening: Check in, drop bags, head to South Congress for dinner and a stroll.
  • Late: Catch live music — check who's playing on 6th Street or at a listening room. Austin's calendar is stacked most weekends.

Saturday

The full Austin day

  • Morning: Breakfast tacos. Ask any local for their spot — everyone has one and they're all a little right.
  • Midday: Barton Springs Pool (cold year-round, go early on weekends) or a walk around Lady Bird Lake.
  • Afternoon: East Austin for food and murals, or South Congress for shopping.
  • Sunset: Congress Avenue Bridge — the bat colony emergence (roughly March–October) is genuinely worth seeing once.
  • Dinner: Book ahead. The good spots fill up on Saturdays — this is the #1 thing visitors get wrong.

Sunday

Brunch + one last thing

  • Brunch: Go early or expect a wait. Austin does brunch seriously.
  • Before you leave: One last stop — a coffee shop, a record store, or a second swim if it's hot.
